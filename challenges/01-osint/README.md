# 01 — Find the Ghost Engineer

- **Category:** OSINT
- **Planned points:** 100
- **Prerequisite:** Campaign introduction
- **Output:** Station identifier and recording timestamp

## Player objective
Correlate fictional public-facing records to identify which station and recording are relevant to the incident.

## Planned evidence
- Fictional staff directory and engineering posts
- Archived maintenance notice
- Synthetic public announcements
- Repository snapshot or metadata export
- At least one evidence-based decoy

## Acceptance criteria
- All evidence is bundled locally and works offline.
- The answer requires correlation between independent sources.
- Timestamps specify the campaign timezone.
- The stage output selects the correct audio artifact for Stage 02.

Design only. Artifact files, generator, hints, and automated checks are pending.
