# Solver Guide

Welcome to DEAD AIR. Your task is to reconstruct a fictional cyber incident by correlating evidence across eight stages.

## Rules and scope
- Work only with artifacts and services provided by this campaign.
- Do not target external systems or real emergency infrastructure.
- Do not attempt to access organizer systems, other players' data, or files outside the challenge scope.
- Do not publish flags, complete solutions, or unreleased challenge artifacts during an active event unless the organizer permits it.

## Investigation method
1. Read the scenario and record every identifier, timestamp, version, and hash.
2. Preserve original artifacts; analyze copies when practical.
3. Record tool names, commands, observations, and output hashes.
4. Correlate at least two independent sources before treating a finding as fact.
5. Keep a timeline and distinguish observation from inference.
6. Follow the documented stage dependencies rather than guessing flags.
7. Use hints progressively if blocked.
8. Submit only evidence you can explain and support.
