# Story Canon — DEAD AIR: The Last Broadcast

## Premise
Pelican Coastal Resilience Network (PCRN) is a fictional operator of coastal sensors, sirens, and emergency broadcast equipment on a fictional island. At 02:17 AM, its simulated network issues an evacuation message for a nonexistent tsunami.

Senior radio engineer Dr. Ishan Perera had been investigating a suspicious firmware update. Shortly before the incident, he left a cryptic note:

> If the silence begins, don't trust the signal. The record is still there, but not where you expect it.

He cannot be interviewed, so players must reconstruct the incident from preserved evidence.

## Narrative truth
The false broadcast is the final visible event, not the original compromise. The investigation should reveal a chain involving concealed evidence, incomplete maintenance records, a narrowly scoped authorization flaw, a firmware integrity failure, and an unauthorized application-level command.

The exact technical root cause and final evidence schema belong in organizer-only solution material once implemented. Player-facing story text must not reveal the answer directly.

## Canonical identifiers
- Organization: Pelican Coastal Resilience Network (PCRN)
- Engineer: Dr. Ishan Perera
- Station: STATION-09
- Incident time: 02:17 AM, fictional local campaign time
- Primary event: unauthorized evacuation broadcast
- Suspicious component: firmware/update path (specific artifact to be defined and verified during implementation)

## Tone
Investigative, technical, tense, and evidence-led. The narrative should encourage players to compare independent sources and challenge assumptions rather than trust a single artifact.

## Consistency rules
- Use a documented timezone for every timestamp.
- Keep names, station IDs, firmware versions, hashes, and evidence IDs consistent across all generated artifacts.
- All infrastructure and data are synthetic.
- Decoys must be plausible but rejectable through evidence.
- Never base clues on real people, real emergency incidents, or real operational infrastructure.
